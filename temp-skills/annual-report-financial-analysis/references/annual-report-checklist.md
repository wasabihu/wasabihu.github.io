# Annual Report Checklist

Use this checklist when the user wants a more complete review than a quick summary.

## Core extraction

- Reporting period and prior comparison period
- Revenue, gross profit, operating income, net income
- Operating cash flow, capex, free cash flow
- Cash, total debt, current assets, current liabilities
- Shares outstanding, dividends, buybacks

## Questions to answer

1. Is growth broad-based or driven by a single segment, geography, or one-time item?
2. Are margins improving because of operating leverage, price, mix, or temporary effects?
3. Does net income convert into cash flow, or is earnings quality weak?
4. Is leverage manageable given cash generation and maturity profile?
5. Did working capital help or hurt cash flow, and is that sustainable?
6. Are there impairment charges, restructurings, or accounting changes that distort comparability?
7. Do management statements in MD&A match the financial statements?
8. What are the top 3 risks that could hurt the next reporting period?

## Suggested deliverables

- Short investment memo
- Ratio table with commentary
- Red-flag summary
- Year-over-year trend summary
